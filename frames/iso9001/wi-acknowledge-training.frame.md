# WI: acknowledge training

For: anyone holding the `staff` role.

1. When a controlled document becomes effective and your role is in its
   training roles, an item "Acknowledge the document" appears in your
   **inbox** with a due date 14 days out. The record is TRN-NNNNN in
   Quality/Training and waits for you alone.
2. Open the record page, follow the link to the document, and read the
   revision named on the record.
3. Return to the record page and decide **approve**; in the meaning field
   write that you have read and understood the revision. That decision is
   your signature; the record closes as `acknowledged`.
4. If you do not understand the document, do not approve. Message the
   document's author from the record page, or decide **reject** with the
   reason; the quality manager will follow up.
5. Items not acknowledged by the due date are escalated to the quality
   manager at the next tick.

## Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Staff | Acknowledges each revision they are trained on, as this instruction says. |
